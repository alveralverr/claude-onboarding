# Cowork Practice Kit

These are **safe, made-up sample files** for practicing Claude Cowork. None of this is real client data — every name, company, email, and number here is invented. Practice on these freely, then delete them whenever you like.

Files in this kit:

- `sample-client-inbox.md` — a fake inbox with a handful of client emails to triage and reply to.
- `sample-meeting-notes.md` — messy notes from a fake team meeting, with action items buried inside.
- `sample-receipts.md` — a disorganized list of expenses to extract into a clean table.
- `sample-week.md` — a fake calendar week for practicing availability summaries (no real calendar needed).

Why a practice kit? So you can do *real* Cowork tasks — triage, drafting, extraction, summarizing — without ever pointing Claude at sensitive or client information while you're still finding your footing.
