# Lesson 7 — How a Cowork task runs

## Learning objectives

- Understand why Cowork is your default mode at Magic.
- Know the step-by-step loop of how a Cowork task runs.
- Understand the difference between serial (Chat) and parallel (Cowork) work.

## The mode where Claude stops answering and starts doing

Cowork is the agentic mode in Claude Desktop — it runs on your computer and completes work across your files, browser, and tools. **As a Magic assistant, always use Cowork as your default mode.**

### Always use Cowork

Your job is task execution. Cowork is how Claude executes. Use it whenever:

- You have a clear deliverable or outcome.
- The work touches your files or connected tools.
- You'd rather hand off the job and review the output.
- You're running recurring tasks on a schedule.

### Chat vs Cowork

- **Chat is serial** — you ask, follow up, and refine step by step, and *you* assemble the output.
- **Cowork is parallel** — you give one goal and it reads files, searches Drive, and drafts the report at once, then delivers finished work to your folder. *It* assembles the output.

The lesson: **always use Cowork.**

## How a Cowork task runs — delegate, but stay in control

**1. You delegate the work.** Send a prompt describing the objective — what you need done and what *done* looks like.

**2. Claude takes it from there:**

- **Understand** — asks clarifying questions to make sure it's clear on your goal.
- **Plan** — breaks the work into steps you can see in the sidebar.
- **Execute** — works across your files, tools, and the web.
- **Verify** — checks its own output for quality and accuracy.
- **Deliver** — produces finished files and a summary of what it did.

**3. You come back to completed work.** Finished files in your folder, actions taken in your tools, and a record of everything that happened.

> Every action appears in the sidebar. **Claude pauses and asks for your approval before doing anything consequential on your working folder.**

## What it looks like in practice (use cases)

- Hand Claude a pile of receipts, invoices, or screenshots. Come back to a formatted spreadsheet with all the data extracted and organized.
- Give Claude your templates and source materials. It produces polished docs, decks, or reports — and can run on a schedule to keep them current.

## Knowledge check

**Question (multiple choice):** What does Claude do *before* taking any consequential action on your working folder?

- A) Nothing — it just acts
- B) It pauses and asks for your approval, and every action appears in the sidebar
- C) It emails your AL
- D) It switches models

**Correct answer: B.** Claude pauses and asks for approval before doing anything consequential on your working folder, and every action appears in the sidebar so you stay in control.

*(If wrong: re-explain "delegate, but stay in control" — you can watch every action in the sidebar, and Claude asks before consequential changes. Then re-ask.)*

**Question (scenario):** A trainee asks why they should use Cowork instead of just chatting back and forth to build a report. What's the core difference?

*Answer:* Chat is serial — you drive every step and assemble the output yourself. Cowork is parallel — you give one goal and it reads files, searches sources, and drafts the report at once, then delivers finished work to your folder. As a Magic assistant, Cowork is your default.

## Reflection

Ask the trainee: *Think of the multi-tool task you mentioned earlier. Walk me through what "done" would look like for it — what files or actions would you expect back?* This reinforces describing the objective and the finished state.
