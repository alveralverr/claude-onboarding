# Lesson 4 — Setup ① Accept your invite + ② Desktop app & project folder

Work through the steps in order and tick each item as you go. Progress saves automatically.

## Step 1 — Accept your invite (~3 min)

Checklist:

- Get your webmail credentials from your ALs.
- Go to **magicassistant.ai/webmail** and login using your new webmail username and password.
- Open your inbox and find the Claude invite link.
- Click **Accept invite** and follow the steps.
- Confirm you can see the Claude homepage.

> **Heads up.** The invite may expire without notice. If this happens and you need access, email **product-team@getmagicea.com**.

## Step 2 — Download the desktop app and open Cowork (~8 min)

The desktop app unlocks **Cowork** — Claude's ability to access your files and execute tasks, not just answer questions.

### Installation

- Go to **claude.ai/download** (or follow the link on your first sign-in).
- Download for your OS — Mac or Windows.
- Open the installer and follow the steps.
- Sign in with the **same account** from step one.

### Getting started with Cowork

- When the desktop app opens, switch to **Cowork** in the mode picker (left sidebar).
- In your first Cowork session, type `/setup-cowork` and press enter to run guided setup.

### Connect your project folder

- Select **Work in a project** in the chat bar to pick where Claude reads context from and saves finished work back to.
- Choose an existing folder or create a new project — we recommend a dedicated *Magic Work* or *Claude Tasks* folder. Avoid pointing it at your Desktop or Downloads.

**A folder** — A folder on your computer. Cowork reads what's there and saves new files alongside it. Scope it to one piece of work, or point it at a broader directory several tasks share.

**A project** — A workspace that carries its own files, instructions, and memory across sessions. Create one in Cowork's sidebar, or import a project you already use in Chat.

> **Keep the desktop app open while you work.** Cowork only runs through the desktop app, not the browser. The folder you select is Claude's workspace where it can read, write, or delete files.
