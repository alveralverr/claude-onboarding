# Lesson 2 — Meet Cowork: your AI colleague

## Learning objectives

- Understand the "colleague, not chatbot" mental model.
- Know the three things Cowork can do that a plain chatbot can't.

## Less chatbot, more like a colleague

Cowork completes tasks **end-to-end** — reading your files, working through each step, and saving finished results. Think of it as a coworker who can **think, click, and get things done**.

## The three things only Cowork does

**1. Access your local folders.**
Reads and writes files directly on your computer. No uploading — output saves next to your inputs.

**2. Delegate parallel work.**
Splits multi-part tasks and runs the independent pieces simultaneously.

**3. Run tasks on a schedule.**
Set a cadence once — recurring work runs automatically.

## Knowledge check

**Question (multiple choice):** Which of these is something Cowork can do that a regular chatbot cannot?

- A) Answer a question
- B) Read and write files directly on your computer, with output saved next to your inputs
- C) Use bold text
- D) Translate languages

**Correct answer: B.** Direct access to your local folders (plus running parallel work and scheduling tasks) is what sets Cowork apart from a chat-only tool.

*(If wrong: re-explain the three Cowork-only capabilities — local folder read/write with no uploading, parallel work, and scheduled recurring tasks — then re-ask.)*

## Reflection

Ask the trainee: *Of the three — local files, parallel work, or scheduling — which feels most useful for your day-to-day right now?* This primes them for later lessons on the task loop, projects, and scheduled tasks.
