# Lesson 1 — What's different about Claude

## Learning objectives

- Understand what Claude Cowork is and how it differs from MagicGPT and ChatGPT.
- Know what's included in your Magic benefit.
- Be able to read the comparison between MagicGPT, ChatGPT, and Claude.

## Your Magic benefit

As a Magic assistant, you get access to a **Claude Team Standard seat worth ₱1,500/month**.

That seat includes:

- **Claude Cowork**
- **Your local files as context**
- **Latest frontier models**
- **All Magic skills**
- **App Connectors**
- **Claude Design**

Claude Cowork is Magic's new **primary AI tool**. This course takes you from your invite email to your first real task. *Complete every step before using Claude for client work.*

## The one thing that changes how you work

**Claude does things, not just answers requests.**

You may have used MagicGPT and ChatGPT. Claude sits in the same place — you chat, it answers — with one difference that changes how you'll work day to day:

> **Claude Cowork runs on your own machine, with your real files and tools.** It is built for the work you do, with pre-built Magic skill templates ready from day one.

## How it stacks up — MagicGPT vs ChatGPT vs Claude

| Capability | MagicGPT | ChatGPT | Claude |
|---|---|---|---|
| Works in your browser | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Works on your desktop | — | Companion window | Full desktop app |
| Executes multi-step tasks | — | Cloud sandbox, capped monthly | On your machine, built for sustained work |
| Works with your real files | Upload or read your screen | Upload or read your screen | Reads and writes across your folders |
| Connected tools (Gmail · Calendar · Drive · Notion · Slack · Asana) | — | Mostly read-only, one source at a time | Reads and takes action, across sources |
| Tuned for EA work | General | Consumer tasks | Pre-built Magic skill templates |

The takeaway: Claude is the only one of the three that runs as a **full desktop app**, works directly with your **real files across folders**, takes **action across multiple connected tools**, and ships with **pre-built Magic skill templates** tuned for EA work.

## Knowledge check

**Question (multiple choice):** What is the single biggest difference between Claude Cowork and tools like MagicGPT or ChatGPT?

- A) It writes longer answers
- B) It runs on your own machine with your real files and tools, and executes tasks rather than just answering
- C) It only works in the browser
- D) It is cheaper

**Correct answer: B.** Claude doesn't just answer — it *does things*, working on your computer with your real files and connected tools.

*(If wrong: re-explain that Claude sits in the same chat-style place as MagicGPT/ChatGPT, but the key shift is that it runs on your own machine with your real files and tools and executes multi-step work end-to-end. Then re-ask.)*
