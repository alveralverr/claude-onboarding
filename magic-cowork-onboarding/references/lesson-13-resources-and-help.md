# Lesson 13 — Learning resources, certification & getting help

## Go beyond the basics

Anthropic's official resources. For a beginner-friendly overview, start with Claude for Work — then use these to deepen your skills and earn your Claude certification.

### Start here

- **Claude 101** (Course) — What Claude is, and organizing your work with projects, artifacts, and skills.
  https://anthropic.skilljar.com/claude-101
- **AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations** (Course · Certificate) — How AI works, ethical grounding, and how to use it responsibly. Aligned with Magic's Claude Certified program.
  https://anthropic.skilljar.com/ai-fluency-framework-foundations
- **Introduction to Claude Cowork** (Course · Free · ~1 hr) — The official Cowork course — task loop, skills, file and research workflows, steering multi-step work.
  https://anthropic.skilljar.com/introduction-to-claude-cowork
- **Introduction to Skills** (Course) — Learn what Skills are, how they work, and even how to create your own skill.
  https://anthropic.skilljar.com/introduction-to-agent-skills

### Go deeper

- **All courses** (Library) — Structured learning paths with video lessons and assessments.
  https://claude.com/resources/courses
- **All tutorials** (Library) — Step-by-step guides for specific Claude features and workflows.
  https://claude.com/resources/tutorials
- **Customize Claude Cowork** (Tutorial) — Tailor Cowork to your working style and recurring task patterns.
  https://claude.com/resources/tutorials/customize-claude-cowork

  https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13837440-use-plugins-in-claude

## Getting help — pick the issue, we'll point to the right person

Use this router to send a trainee to the right contact for their problem:

| What's wrong | Who to contact | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Can't accept invite or sign in | **Product Team** | Can't accept the invite or sign in? Email the Product Team — product-team@getmagicea.com. |
| Can't see Magic templates or Skills | **Product Team** | Usually a permissions issue. Email the Product Team (product-team@getmagicea.com). |
| Google account not connecting | **Connectors, then your AL** | Check Connectors in Settings first, then message your Account Lead. |
| Claude refuses a task | **Your Account Lead** | Try rephrasing first — see the Prompting lesson. Still stuck? Ask your AL. |
| Hit the rate limit | **Wait, or step down to Sonnet** | Hit your Opus limit? Wait for the reset, or switch to Sonnet for the rest of the task — don't drop to Haiku for client work. Learn to manage your tokens for urgent tasks. |
| General Claude questions | **Your Account Lead** | General Claude questions go to your AL first. |
| Technical issue — extension or app | **Product Team** | Technical issues with the extension or desktop app go to the Product Team (product-team@getmagicea.com). |

## Knowledge check

**Question (multiple choice):** Claude refuses a task you asked for. What's the first thing to try, and who do you escalate to?

- A) Email the Product Team immediately
- B) Try rephrasing first (add context, see the Prompting lesson); if still stuck, ask your Account Lead
- C) Switch to Haiku
- D) Reinstall the app

**Correct answer: B.** Rephrase/add context first; if it still won't work, your Account Lead is the contact for a refused task.

*(If wrong: re-explain the routing — refused tasks and general questions go to your AL first; invite/sign-in, missing templates/skills, and technical app issues go to the Product Team; Google connection issues start with Connectors then your AL. Then re-ask.)*
