# Lesson 11 — Choosing the right model

## Learning objectives

- Know that Opus is your default for all EA work.
- Know when to step down to Sonnet, and the rare case for Haiku.
- Know what to do if you hit Sonnet limits.

## The rule: Start on Opus. Step down only when limits push you.

Opus is your default for all EA work — best quality, richest output, strongest reasoning. Switch to Sonnet only if you're hitting Opus rate limits.

### Pick by situation

- **Everyday EA work — the default → Opus.** Your default. Best quality and reasoning for all EA work — research, writing, Cowork, complex tasks. Start here and only step down if rate limits actually stop you.
- **Hitting Opus rate limits → Sonnet.** Still strong for most work, lighter on quota.
- **High-volume extraction or very quick summaries (rare) → Haiku.**

### Model reference table

| Model | Use for | Avoid for |
|---|---|---|
| **Opus** | Everything by default — all EA work, research, writing, Cowork, complex reasoning. | Nothing. Start here unless rate limits stop you. |
| **Sonnet** | When you've hit your Opus rate limit. Still strong for most work, lighter on quota. | Accuracy-critical or complex multi-step tasks where quality matters most. |
| **Haiku** | High-volume rote data only — bulk categorization, simple extraction at scale. | Almost everything. Never use for reasoning, writing, or anything needing judgement. |

> **Hitting Sonnet limits?** Don't drop to Haiku — defaulting to a weaker model may affect the quality of results. Escalate to your account lead so your quota can be reviewed.

## Knowledge check

**Question (multiple choice):** You've hit your Opus rate limit mid-task. What's the right move?

- A) Drop straight to Haiku
- B) Switch to Sonnet — still strong for most work, lighter on quota — and escalate to your AL if you hit Sonnet limits too
- C) Stop working for the day
- D) Reinstall the app

**Correct answer: B.** Step down to Sonnet when Opus limits stop you. Don't drop to Haiku for client work; if Sonnet limits hit too, escalate to your AL to review your quota.

*(If wrong: re-explain the ladder — Opus is the default for all EA work; Sonnet only when Opus limits push you; Haiku is for high-volume rote data only and should not be used for reasoning, writing, or judgement. Then re-ask.)*

**Question (scenario):** A trainee says Haiku is fastest so they'll just use it for everything to save quota. What do you tell them?

*Answer:* Avoid that. Haiku is for high-volume rote data only (bulk categorization, simple extraction at scale) and should never be used for reasoning, writing, or anything needing judgement. Defaulting to a weaker model hurts result quality.

## Reflection

Ask the trainee: *Which model will you start every task on, and what's the one trigger that makes you switch?* (Expected: start on Opus; switch to Sonnet only on Opus rate limits.)
