# Lesson 5 — Setup ③ Connect your apps

## Why connect apps

Claude connects to popular apps — Microsoft 365, Notion, Slack, Canva, Asana, HubSpot, QuickBooks, and many more — so you can bring agentic AI into your real workflows. **Cowork's real power comes from connecting the apps your client already uses.**

## Steps to add a connector

- In the desktop app, click **Customize** (left sidebar).
- Go to **Connectors** and click **Add Connector** (the plus sign).
- Find **Google Workspace apps** and add them.
- The added app appears in your Connectors list, under Not Connected.
- Click **Connect** and sign in with your Google account to allow permissions.
- Repeat for **Gmail**, **Google Calendar**, **Google Drive**, or other apps.

## Test it

If you're connected to your calendar, open a new Cowork session and type:

> `What meetings do I have tomorrow?`

If Claude lists your calendar events, you're connected. If it says it doesn't have access, go back to Connectors and check.

> ⚠️ **Connect your own Google account only.** Never connect a client's email or calendar without their explicit permission.

## Knowledge check

**Question (multiple choice):** After adding a Google connector, what's the quickest way to confirm it actually works?

- A) Restart your computer
- B) Open a new Cowork session and ask something that uses it — e.g. "What meetings do I have tomorrow?"
- C) Reinstall the app
- D) Email the Product Team

**Correct answer: B.** Test a connector by asking Claude to use it. If it lists your calendar events, you're connected; if it says it doesn't have access, recheck Connectors.

*(If wrong: re-explain the test-it flow and that a "doesn't have access" response means going back to Connectors to check the connection. Then re-ask.)*
