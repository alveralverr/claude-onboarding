# Lesson 6 — Setup ④ Magic skill templates + ⑤ Your first task

## Step 4 — Find your Magic skill templates (~4 min)

Magic made pre-built skill templates for the most common EA tasks. **Always start from a template for related tasks.**

Available templates:

- **Email Management**
- **Calendar Management**
- **Writing**
- *(more coming soon)*

Steps to add a skill:

- In the desktop app, click **Customize** (left sidebar).
- Go to **Skills** and click **Add Skill** (the plus sign), then **Browse Skills**.
- Navigate to the **Your organization** tab and **add the skill** available to you.
- Confirm that the skill has been added to your **skills list**.

How to use a skill once it's added:

- Use it in Cowork by typing its slash command — for example `/email-management` or `/calendar-management` — then combine it with your own prompt.
- Sometimes, Claude chooses the right skill automatically if the task is clear, without you having to use the slash commands.
- You can even ask Claude to explain what a specific skill does and how to use it.

> If you don't see any skill, message your Account Lead or email the Product Team — there may be a permissions issue on your account.

## Step 5 — Try your first Cowork task (~10 min)

Open a new Cowork session and pick something real from your to-do list — ideally a task that spans several tools or steps.

### How to run it

1. Set the **working folder** to send with your prompt.
2. Type clear, **specific instructions** for what you need.
3. If Claude shows a plan or asks **clarifying questions**, approve it if it's right — redirect if not.
4. Watch it work, or step away and come back.
5. **Always review** the output before sending anything to a client.
6. If it's off, **re-prompt** until you get the outcome you want.

### Sample first tasks

Link the right folders, files, connectors, or skills first — always think "what does the AI need to know".

- "Go through my inbox, find unread client emails from the last 24 hours, and draft a reply for each one that needs a response. Save the drafts to my Magic Work folder."
- "Check my Google Calendar for next week and write a summary of my client's availability. Save it as availability.md in my folder."
- "Research [company name] — their industry, recent news, and key people. Write a one-page briefing and save it to my folder."
- "Summarize these meeting notes and pull out every action item with the owner and deadline."

> **To get more practice,** try doing tasks in Cowork using the connectors or skills for your real-world tasks.

Final checklist item: *Ran my first real Cowork task and reviewed the output.*

## Knowledge check

**Question (multiple choice):** Before sending any Cowork output to a client, what must you always do?

- A) Nothing — Claude is always correct
- B) Review the output yourself, and re-prompt if it's off
- C) Delete the working folder
- D) Switch to Haiku

**Correct answer: B.** Always review the output before sending anything to a client, and re-prompt until you get the outcome you want.

*(If wrong: re-explain that step 5 of "how to run it" is "always review the output before sending anything to a client," and step 6 is to re-prompt if it's off. Then re-ask.)*
